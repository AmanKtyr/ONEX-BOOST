01

Introduction & Scope

Welcome to ONEX BOOST, engineered and operated by HELYONEX ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting the personal data and operational confidentiality of fitness business owners, gym administrators, trainers, and fitness center members.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website (onexboost.com), the ONEX BOOST cloud management application, mobile member portals, and our hardware IoT sync services (including turnstiles and biometric attendance devices).

Core Privacy Principle: HELYONEX does not sell, rent, or monetize your gym's member lists or revenue metrics to third-party advertisers or competitors. Your data is exclusively used to operate, automate, and secure your gym.
02

Information We Collect

To provide end-to-end gym operating software, we collect information under the following categories:

  • Gym Profile & Billing Data: Gym trade name, GSTIN, owner contact details, business bank account or UPI ID for payout settlement, and billing address.
  • Member & Lead Information: Names, mobile numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, emergency contacts, subscription dates, assigned trainers, and plan history inputted by your gym staff.
  • Attendance & Check-In Logs: Timestamps of member check-ins, check-outs, QR scans, and turnstile gate access events.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, operating system version, and system diagnostic logs to ensure zero-downtime performance.
03

How We Use Information

We process information solely to fulfill our contractual service obligations to your fitness business:

  • Automating membership expirations, renewal reminders, and GST invoice generation.
  • Dispatching automated WhatsApp notifications for payment receipts, attendance alerts, and promotional announcements configured by the gym administrator.
  • Controlling turnstile locks and biometric gates in sub-second latency based on valid membership status.
  • Providing real-time analytics dashboards (revenue collected, pending dues, member retention rate, and trainer commission logs).
04

Biometric & Hardware Security

ONEX BOOST integrates seamlessly with biometric attendance machines (e.g., eSSL, ZKTeco, Realtime) and facial recognition turnstiles.

Tokenized Storage Only: Biometric readers convert biological prints or facial geometry into non-reversible mathematical template strings. These templates are stored strictly in local device firmware and secure encrypted tokens. Raw optical fingerprint scans or unhashed facial images are never transmitted or stored on cloud servers.

When a member leaves or cancels their membership, their biometric enrollment token can be automatically wiped from all connected turnstiles with a single click from the ONEX BOOST dashboard.

05

Billing & Payment Security

All online fee payments (UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, wallets) initiated through ONEX BOOST are processed via RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways (such as Razorpay, PayU, and Cashfree).

  • ONEX BOOST servers never store, inspect, or retain raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank net-banking passwords.
  • We store only high-level payment confirmation metadata (Transaction ID, payment mode, amount, date, GST breakdown, and settlement status).
06

Data Protection & Backups

HELYONEX implements rigorous organizational, physical, and technical controls compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):

  • Transport & Rest Encryption: TLS 1.3 protocol encryption for all data in flight; AES-256 encrypted database volumes at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Gym staff (receptionists, trainers, managers) can only view information relevant to their assigned permissions. Sensitive financial reports can be restricted strictly to gym owners.
  • Daily Automated Backups: Automated multi-region encrypted snapshots guarantee rapid recovery against accidental loss or hardware failure.
07

Data Ownership & Rights

As a gym founder or authorized administrative account holder, you hold sovereign control over your business data:

  • Right to Export: Export all member directories, billing history, and attendance records into standard Excel / CSV spreadsheets at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (De-registration): Request permanent deletion of your gym's tenant records upon cancellation of your subscription.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or correct any inaccurate member profiles directly from the administrative portal.
