Our
Story

We spent months on the gym floor across Lucknow, Delhi NCR, and tier-1/tier-2 Indian cities shadowing gym owners, receptionists, and trainers. We witnessed passionate fitness coaches buried under WhatsApp chats, paper registers, broken biometric thumb scanners, and uncollected dues.

Generic overseas software was clunky, exorbitant, and completely neglected Indian payment methods, GST compliance, and local WhatsApp workflows. That is why HELYONEX engineered ONEX BOOST: a bespoke, razor-sharp operating system built from the ground up for Indian fitness facilities.
