What is OneX-Fitness?

OneX-Fitness is an all-in-one gym management software designed to streamline and automate the daily operations of gyms, fitness studios, and personal trainers. It handles everything from memberships and billing to attendance and trainer workflows.

Is OneX-Fitness suitable for small gyms?

Absolutely. OneX-Fitness is built to scale, making it a perfect fit for both small, independent gyms and large, multi-location fitness centers. Our flexible plans are designed to meet the needs of any size business.

How does the billing and payment system work?

Our software automates invoicing, tracks due payments, and provides clear financial visibility. It simplifies the entire collection process, reducing manual effort and ensuring you get paid on time.

Can I manage trainers and their schedules?

Yes, OneX-Fitness includes powerful tools for managing trainer profiles, assigning personal training sessions, tracking attendance, and organizing schedules, giving you complete control over your team.

Data security is our top priority. We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to ensure that your business and member data is always protected.
