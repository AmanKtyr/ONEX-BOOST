Complete Gym Operational Lifecycle & System Map
How OneX-Fitness connects leads, admissions, payments, QR check-ins, PT coaching, and financials.
Open Dashboard
The 6-Stage Gym Operational Pipeline
1
Lead Capture & Enquiry Nurturing

Walk-in or social lead recorded → Trial batch scheduled → Follow-up logged → Click Convert to Member to transfer all contact data automatically.

2
Member Admission & Plan Assignment

Webcam profile photo captured → 6-digit Pincode auto-fills City/State → Select Membership Plan → Unique Member ID (MEM-YYYY-XXXX) issued.

3
Billing, Payment & GST Invoicing

System calculates Package Fee + Registration + CGST/SGST → Accept Cash, UPI QR, or Card → Print GST Tax Invoice → Pending amounts route to Submit Due.

4
Daily Anti-Proxy QR Check-In & Coaching

Member punches in via 4-digit dynamic QR screen → Dedicated Trainer Portal assigns custom workout & diet routines → Temporary leave freezing.

5
Inventory Sales & Member Engagement

Sell protein/supplements via Inventory Stock Out → Host bootcamps & fitness competitions via Events with public registration links.

6
Executive Financial Analytics & P&L Reports

All collections balance against operating expenses (Rent, Salaries, Maintenance) to calculate real-time Net Profit with exportable CSV spreadsheets.

Member Registration & Onboarding Walkthrough
1
Profile Info & Live Webcam Snap

Input Name, Mobile, Email, Gender, and DOB. Use the live webcam button to snap an immediate passport photo or upload from disk.

2
Postal Pincode Auto-Lookup

Enter the 6-digit Indian Pincode. The system calls the national postal directory to automatically populate City, State, and Area.

3
Plan Selection & Fee Calculation

Select the Membership Package (e.g. 3 Months, 6 Months, Annual). Enter registration fees and discounts; CGST/SGST taxes compute dynamically.

4
Initial Payment & Tax Invoice

Record initial payment (Full or Partial). The system immediately generates an official GST Tax Invoice and assigns the unique Member ID.

Member IDs follow the standard enterprise pattern: MEM-YYYY-XXXX (e.g., MEM-2026-0042).

This format guarantees unique, sequential identification per gym branch and integrates seamlessly into QR check-in and tax invoices.

Open the member's profile from Members List. In the membership status card, click Freeze Membership.

Specify the freeze start date, return date, and reason (e.g. travel or medical injury). The system pauses check-in access and automatically shifts the membership expiry date forward by the exact number of frozen days upon unfreezing.

Navigate to Members List and filter by Expired.

Click Renew on the member's row. Select a new package or upgrade their existing plan, record the renewal fee, and the system instantly generates a new Tax Invoice and updates their valid-until date.

Members log in at the main login screen using their assigned credentials. They are routed directly to /portal/member/ to view check-in streaks, active plan validity, workout routines, diet charts, and invoice receipts.

Due Payment Collection Workflow
1
Locate Pending Balance

Open Billing → Submit Due. Filter members with outstanding dues or search by member name or phone.

2
Input Payment Amount

Click Pay Due. Enter the collected amount (system handles both partial installment payments and full clearance).

3
Choose Payment Mode

Select Cash, UPI QR code transfer, or Card. The payment timestamp and collecting staff user are logged for financial audit.

4
Instant Receipt Generation

The system recalibrates the member's remaining due, updates the invoice ledger, and produces a printable payment receipt.

  1. Go to Billing → Submit Due.
  2. Locate the member using their name, phone, or Member ID.
  3. Click Pay Due beside their pending record.
  4. Enter the received payment amount and select payment method (Cash, UPI, or Card).
  5. Submit to instantly update their balance and print the updated payment receipt.

If GST is enabled in General Settings, invoices automatically split tax calculations:

  • Intra-State: 9% CGST + 9% SGST (Total 18%).
  • Inter-State: 18% Integrated IGST.

Your Gym GSTIN, Legal Trade Name, and SAC code (999723 for fitness services) print directly on all member invoices.

Deleting an invoice performs a safe soft-delete. It is immediately routed to Invoice Trash, preventing accidental financial loss. Gym Admins can inspect and restore it with 1 click or choose permanent deletion.

The bank name, account holder, account number, IFSC code, and payment QR code come directly from Payment Settings. Updating them there updates all newly generated invoices immediately.

Anti-Proxy 4-Digit PIN Attendance Flow
1
Display Kiosk Terminal

Launch /attendance/qr-code/ on a front desk tablet or display facing gym entrants.

2
Scan with Smartphone Camera

Member or Trainer scans the displayed QR code using their mobile phone camera or scanner.

3
4-Digit Security PIN Verification

Member enters their personal 4-digit security PIN. This strictly prevents friends or teammates from punching attendance remotely.

4
Immediate Timestamp & Streak Counter

System validates the punch, logs entry time, plays a confirmation chime, and increments the member's active workout streak.

A static QR code can be photographed and shared over WhatsApp, allowing members to check in from home. OneX-Fitness eliminates this via two security layers:

  1. Dynamic Auto-Refresh: The desk QR code refreshes periodically.
  2. 4-Digit PIN Authentication: Each member must enter their secret 4-digit security PIN upon scanning to confirm physical identity.

Go to Attendance → Attendance Report. Select your desired date range and click Export CSV for complete monthly spreadsheets.

Retail Sales (Stock Out) & Maintenance Flow
1
Supplement / Retail Checkout

When a member buys protein powder or a shaker bottle, navigate to Stock Out. Select the item, enter the quantity, and choose the paying member.

2
Real-Time Stock Decrement

The system instantly decrements the remaining stock count, flags low-stock warnings if below threshold, and routes the revenue to the Business Report.

3
Equipment Servicing Scheduling

When gym machines require lubrication, cable tightening, or motor maintenance, create a service entry in Maintenance Log to track repair costs and vendor warranties.

Consumables (Supplements, apparel): Track items sold to customers. Recording a Stock Out decreases stock and logs retail revenue.

Fixed Equipment (Treadmills, Dumbbells): Capital gym assets that do not get sold. They are tracked for preventive servicing and repairs in the Maintenance Log.

1
Aggregate Collections

Membership admissions + Renewal fees + Due collections + PT coaching fees + Supplement retail POS sales.

2
Deduct Operating Overheads

Facility rent + Electricity bills + Staff salaries + Trainer commissions + Equipment maintenance.

3
