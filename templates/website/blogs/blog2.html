2. Top Operational Pain Points Lucknow Gym Owners Face

  • Uncollected Membership Dues: Members often continue working out for weeks after their plans expire simply because staff forgot to check renewal dates.
  • Proxy Attendance & Crowding: Lack of automated turnstile or QR check-ins leads to unverified guests and peak-hour floor congestion.

  • Trainer Commission Disputes: Calculating personal trainer (PT) sessions and split fees manually leads to staff frustration and accounting errors.
