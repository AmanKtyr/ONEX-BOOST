Why We Started

Born on the Gym Floor, Not an Abstract Boardroom

We spent months shadowing gym owners, reception managers, and trainers across Lucknow, Delhi NCR, and tier-1/tier-2 Indian cities. We witnessed passionate fitness coaches buried under WhatsApp chats, paper registers, broken fingerprint machines, and overdue membership disputes.

Generic ERPs and overseas software were either clunky, exorbitantly expensive, or completely neglected Indian payment methods, GST compliance, and local WhatsApp workflows. That is why HELYONEX engineered ONEX BOOST: a bespoke, razor-sharp operating system built from the ground up for Indian fitness businesses.
